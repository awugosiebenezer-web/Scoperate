# ScopeRate zero-cost growth system

## Positioning

**One line:** ScopeRate turns scope, risk, fees, and taxes into a defensible freelance quote and client-ready estimate—free, private, and without signup.

**Primary audience:** Freelance designers, developers, marketers, writers, video professionals, consultants, and small studios pricing fixed-fee projects.

## Launch copy (post manually; adapt to each community)

### Product Hunt / launch directory

**Name:** ScopeRate

**Tagline:** Price freelance projects without guessing

**Description:** ScopeRate is a free, privacy-first toolkit for freelancers. Break a project into deliverables, price uncertainty and revisions, account for fees and profit, then print a client-ready estimate. It also includes focused hourly-rate, day-rate, deposit, and scope-creep cost calculators. Everything runs in the browser; no signup or client data upload.

### Community feedback post

I built a free project-pricing tool for freelancers who know their hourly rate but still get burned on fixed-fee scope. It itemizes deliverables, adds an explicit uncertainty buffer, accounts for direct costs and payment fees, and prints a scope summary with deposit and revision terms. There is no signup and inputs stay in the browser.

I would value feedback on one question: which assumption do you most often forget when quoting—meetings, revisions, client delays, payment fees, or something else?

Link: https://scoperate-final.vercel.app/

**Posting rule:** Use only where self-promotion is permitted, disclose that you built it, stay to answer questions, and do not repost identical copy across communities.
