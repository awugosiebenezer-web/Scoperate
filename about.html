About ScopeRate

Better project pricing starts with a visible scope.

ScopeRate is a free, privacy-first calculator for freelancers, consultants, and small studios. It turns deliverables, realistic hours, uncertainty, costs, fees, and profit into a client-ready fixed-price estimate.

Why it exists

Freelancers routinely estimate only the production work and absorb discovery, meetings, revisions, and project risk for free. ScopeRate makes those assumptions explicit before a quote leaves your desk.

Our principles

  • Useful before monetized: the complete core calculator is free.
  • Private by default: project data stays in the browser.
  • Transparent: estimates are explained and never presented as guaranteed income or professional advice.
  • Uncluttered: future ads and recommendations will be limited, relevant, and clearly labeled.
