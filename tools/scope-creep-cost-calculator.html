Scope creep makes a fixed fee quietly shrink

On fixed-price work, the fee stays constant while unplanned hours increase. That means every unbilled addition lowers the amount earned per hour. The damage can be easy to miss because the invoice value has not changed.

Unbilled scope cost = max(actual hours − estimated hours, 0) × normal hourly rate

Use the result as an early warning

Review estimated versus actual effort at every milestone. A small variance may be normal estimation error. A repeated pattern of new deliverables, reversed approvals, extra stakeholders, or additional revision rounds is a scope change. Raise it before the work is completed, not after the client assumes it was included.

How to discuss a change order

Keep the conversation factual: name the original deliverable, describe the new request, estimate its cost and schedule effect, then ask for written approval. Avoid surprising a client with retroactive charges when there was no prior notice. A useful sentence is: “I’m happy to add this. It falls outside the approved scope, so I’ll send a change estimate for the additional hours and timeline.”

Prevent the next overrun

  • List quantities, formats, acceptance criteria, and exclusions.
  • Define a revision as one consolidated set of feedback.
  • Price ordinary uncertainty into the fixed fee.
  • Separate major unknowns into paid discovery.
  • Track time even when the client buys a fixed outcome.

Frequently asked questions

What counts as scope creep?

Work beyond the agreed deliverables, quantities, revision limits, assumptions, or acceptance criteria is additional scope. Ordinary mistakes or work required to meet the original agreement are not.

Should I invoice the calculated amount automatically?

No. It is a planning value. Use the contract and agreed change process, communicate before doing extra work, and obtain written approval.

What buffer is reasonable?

Clear, familiar work may need around 10%. New clients or meaningful unknowns may justify 20–35%. Very uncertain work is often safer as paid discovery or hourly work.
