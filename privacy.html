Privacy Policy

Effective: August 19, 2026

ScopeRate is designed to minimize data collection. Calculator inputs and the local activity dashboard are stored in your browser using localStorage. They are not sent to our server.

Information processed

We may receive standard hosting logs such as IP address, browser type, referring page, and request time through our hosting provider for security and reliability. Privacy-friendly Vercel Web Analytics infrastructure is included to count aggregate pages and events without storing project scope content; reporting begins only when enabled in the Vercel project.

Share links

If you create a share link, calculator values are encoded in the URL. Anyone who receives that URL can read those values. Do not put confidential client information in a shared link.

Cookies and advertising

No advertising network is currently active. If one is added after approval, this policy and consent controls will be updated before personalized advertising is enabled where consent is required. Dedicated placeholders do not track or serve ads.

Retention and control

You can clear locally stored inputs and activity through your browser's site-data controls. Hosting log retention is controlled by the hosting provider.

Third parties

Hosting is intended to be provided by Vercel. External links have their own policies. Future affiliate links will be clearly disclosed.

Contact

For privacy requests, use the contact page. This policy may be updated as the service changes.
