Questions freelancers ask

How does ScopeRate calculate a project price?

It multiplies scoped hours by your sustainable rate, applies an uncertainty buffer, adds direct costs, then grosses up for payment fees and your chosen profit margin. Tax reserve is shown separately for planning.

Is my project information uploaded?

No. Calculation and usage history stay in your browser. Share links contain only the calculator values you deliberately encode in the URL.

Should I use the result as a contract?

No. The printable output is an estimate and scope summary, not legal advice or a substitute for a contract reviewed for your jurisdiction.

Why add profit if my hourly rate already pays me?

Your rate compensates your labor and overhead. Profit creates resilience for slow periods, training, equipment replacement, and growth. Set it to zero if profit is already built into your rate.
